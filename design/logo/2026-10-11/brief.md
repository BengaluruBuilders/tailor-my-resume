# TailorMyResume logo brief (2026-10-11)

**Name:** TailorMyResume (one word, exact spelling).
**What:** Android app that turns a candidate's confirmed background and a job description into a tailored
application. It never invents facts.
**Audience:** job seekers on a phone, early to mid career, applying to many roles.
**Adjectives (assumed):** honest, made-to-measure, friendly, quick, confident.
**Competitors:** Teal, Rezi, Kickresume, Jobscan, Enhancv, Resume.io, Zety.
**Category clichés (off-limits without a twist):** document with grey lines, pencil/pen, checkmark, briefcase,
AI sparkle, blue gradient, upward arrow.
**Constraints:** Android adaptive icon (108 dp, 66 dp safe circle) and a monochrome themed icon. The app is
black-first with lime `#A3F43F`; blue `#5AA9F8` and amber `#F7A940` are story colours. Paige, a resume page with
a face, is the in-app mascot. Type: Space Mono and Space Grotesk.
**Current icon:** a placeholder arrow (`app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_launcher_foreground.xml`).

## Word map
tailor → needle, thread, stitch, seam, lapel, collar, tape measure, chalk, pin, made to measure, fit
resume → page, dog-ear, Paige
honest → plain, solid, confirmed, nothing added

## Longlist
1. Lapel page: the resume page wears a notched lapel, dressed for this job.
2. Needle T: a T whose stem is a needle, threaded through its eye.
3. Paige: the mascot reduced to a page with a face.
4. Measured page: tape-measure ticks along a page edge.
5. Stitched fold: a dog-ear held down by one stitch.
6. Seam brackets: two page halves joined by a stitch line.
7. Pin head i: a tailor's pin as the dot in a lowercase mark.
8. Cut-to-fit: a page edge cut to the shape of a job card.

Built: 1, 2, 3 (pictorial, letterform, mascot).
