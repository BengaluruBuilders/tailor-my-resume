What we heard

The brief

An Android app that turns a candidate's confirmed background and a job description into a tailored application, without inventing anything. Job seekers apply to many roles on their phone; the app should feel honest, friendly and quick, not like another grey document tool.

honestmade-to-measurefriendlyquickconfident

Success: reads at 16 px, works in one colour, matches the in-app mascot Paige, stands apart from blue document-and-checkmark resume tools.

Final design

Paige

Paige, the app's own resume page with a face, is the brand: your story, friendly and true.

  • Same character the user meets on sign-in, job paste and tailoring, so the icon and the app tell one story
  • A face on a page is warmer than the category's grey documents, checkmarks and pencils
  • Solid silhouette with real cut-outs: survives 16 px, one colour and the Android themed icon
